Health, Safety & Quality

Safety and compliance built into delivery, not bolted on

Lane BB recognises that safety, quality and regulatory compliance are essential to successful project delivery. We aim to ensure that:

Work is performed according to the approved scope and specifications.
Appropriate safety measures are applied to each assignment.
Qualified or registered professionals are appointed where legally required.
Equipment is inspected and operated responsibly.
Construction and technical work complies with applicable requirements.
Project risks are identified and managed.
Work areas are maintained in a safe and orderly condition.
Clients receive clear communication throughout the project.

Project-specific safety files, method statements and compliance documents can be prepared where required by the scope of work.
