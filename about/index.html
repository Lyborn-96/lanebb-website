About Lane BB

One company. Multiple capabilities.

Lane BB, formerly known as Lane BrandsBridge, is a Johannesburg-based multidisciplinary service provider established to connect clients with practical solutions across technology, infrastructure, events, logistics and branding.

Our Structure

Five specialised divisions, one point of accountability

This structure allows us to serve clients requiring a single specialised service as well as organisations looking for an integrated service provider capable of coordinating several parts of a project.

We understand that government institutions, municipalities, corporate organisations and contractors require more than promises. They need clear communication, proper documentation, dependable coordination and services delivered according to an agreed scope.

Our approach is built around understanding the client's requirements, assembling the right resources and managing each assignment responsibly from planning to completion.
