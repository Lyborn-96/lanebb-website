Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about working with Lane BB

Can we appoint Lane BB for only one service?

Yes. Clients can appoint a single Lane BB division or request an integrated solution involving several divisions.

Does Lane BB work with government institutions?

Yes. Our services are structured for government, municipal and other public-sector requirements, subject to the procurement process and project specifications.

Can Lane BB work outside Johannesburg?

Yes. Lane BB is based in Johannesburg and can provide services in other locations depending on the project requirements.

Do you supply event marquees and other equipment?

We coordinate a broad range of event infrastructure and equipment according to the requirements of each event. Clients can request a complete event-support quotation.

Can you prepare architectural plans?

Yes. Architectural planning services can be coordinated according to the nature of the proposed building, renovation or alteration. Where professional registration or municipal approval is required, the appropriate professionals will be involved.

Can your IT division develop a customised system?

Yes. We assess the organisation's processes and requirements before proposing a suitable application, CRM, integration or automation solution.

Do you provide quotations for tenders?

Yes. We assess the supplied specifications, quantities, location and delivery requirements before preparing a written quotation.
